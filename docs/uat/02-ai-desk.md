# UAT — AI Desk

**Route:** `/ai`  
**Permission:** At least one of: sales, inventory, finance, purchasing, or insights READ

---

## Purpose

AI-generated **daily summary**, **monthly summary**, and **insights board** (fast sellers, slow movers, reorder suggestions, store stock needs, profit hotspots). Numbers come from your company data; narrative is generated by Gemini.

---

## Prerequisites

- `GEMINI_API_KEY` configured on server (owner responsibility).
- Company has sales, purchases, and stock history.
