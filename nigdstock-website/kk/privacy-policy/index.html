Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This privacy policy describes how Abyssinia Software Technology PLC handles information related to the public marketing website at https://nigdstock.com.

Website inquiries

The demo request form on this website does not store submissions on a server operated by this static site. When you submit the form, your device prepares an email addressed to abyssiniasoftwaretechnologyplc@gmail.com. You choose whether to send that email through your own email application. Message content you send by email is handled according to ordinary business communication practices.
